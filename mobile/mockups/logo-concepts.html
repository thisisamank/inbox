Agentic Inbox, logo concepts

Six directions for the app icon. Each one shows the large mark, the iOS rounded square, the Android circle, launcher and notification sizes, a dark home screen, and the Android 13 themed (single-color) icon. Pick one by its number, or combine parts, for example "3 with the spark from 1".

01

Spark envelope

A white envelope on clay orange. The four-point spark is the agent, at the corner where a stamp goes. Direct: "mail, with AI".

Strength: reads at every size. Risk: the most common pairing for AI mail apps.

Agent tray

An orange inbox tray on ink, with the spark dropping into it. The tray is the same icon as the Mail tab, so the app icon and the app agree.

03

Open envelope "A"

An envelope with its flap open. The flap and the body make an "A" for Agentic, and the spark is cut out of the flap.

Strength: most ownable; it is a monogram and a mail icon at once. Risk: the cut-out spark disappears at 24 px.

04

Thread list

Three inbox rows on cream. The top row is orange, and the purple spark marks it: "the agent picked this one for you". It uses the app's agent color.

Strength: says "inbox triage" without an envelope. Risk: looks like a to-do or notes app at a glance.

05

Talk to your inbox

A chat bubble with an envelope inside. It puts the agent conversation first: you ask your inbox, it answers.

Strength: clear story for the agent tab. Risk: close to messaging apps; reads as "chat" more than "mail".

06

Spark @

An "@" whose centre is the spark: an address with an agent inside it. The simplest silhouette of the six.

Strength: bold and very legible at small sizes. Risk: "@" alone is generic; it leans on the spark to feel like this product.

My pick: 03, Open envelope "A". It is the only concept that is both a mail icon and a monogram, so it can become the brand mark and not only an app icon. The cut-out spark is too small at 24 px, so the notification icon would use the flap and body only. Runner-up: 01, the safest choice if recognition matters more than distinctness.
